ANT Live no stick

Plug the ANT USB stick into the Mac and press Connect ANT stick. Then, on the watch, either:

  1. All values: start an activity with the AntLive data field on one of its data screens. The watch then sends heart rate, power, cadence, speed, distance, timer, altitude, ascent, position, heading, calories, averages and maxima, training effect, Pulse Ox, pressure, temperature, battery and steps.
  2. Heart rate only, any Garmin watch, no app: switch the list at the top to ANT+ sensors & heart-rate broadcast and turn on Broadcast Heart Rate (on the FR265: hold UP › Health & Wellness › Wrist Heart Rate › Broadcast Heart Rate; or, for every ride, START › activity › Menu › activity settings › Broadcast Heart Rate).

The stick listens to one of two ANT networks at a time, chosen in the list at the top: Garmin watches (AntLive), or ANT+ sensors & heart-rate broadcast for straps, power meters, speed/cadence sensors, trainers and watches broadcasting heart rate. Every device heard shows up as its own card; several watches at once work too.
