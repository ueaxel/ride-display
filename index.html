Sync video and route Find a spot you can recognise in the video (a crossing, a bridge, a turn), then click that spot on the map.

Ride videos

Put each ride's video (360° equirectangular .mp4 from GoPro Player, or a normal video) and the watch's .fit (or Garmin Connect .zip) of the same ride in one folder. Videos are matched to rides by recording time, or else by the same file name.

Panels

Drag a panel by its title, resize it at the bottom-right corner. Positions are kept on this computer.

Reference ride

Pick the original ride's .fit file, or the .zip straight from Garmin Connect (the activity → ⚙ → Export Original). Rides without a power meter get power estimated from speed, grade and weight, as in tools/estimate_power.py.

Ride Display

Tablet: connect the HR strap and the trainer (both over Bluetooth; the watch keeps the trainer on ANT+), press Start as you start the watch.
Phone: connect the HR strap and GPS, press Start at the spot where the original ride started.
Reset gap lines the ghost up with you again (after a red light or a stop), like the Lap button on the watch.
