Ride videos

Put each ride's video (360° equirectangular .mp4 from GoPro Player, or a normal video) and the watch's .fit (or Garmin Connect .zip) of the same ride in one folder. Videos are matched to rides by recording time, or else by the same file name.

Ride video from a GoPro and a .fit file in one go (GPS sync, 360° conversion, horizon levelling): Ride Video Maker (runs on your computer).

Team rides

More rides of the same route race along as coloured dots on the map and the elevation profile (the numbers stay those of the reference ghost). Each starts where it passes the reference's start and follows the same clock as the ghost (Reset gap moves them all).

Reference:

Settings

Rider and bike: for the power estimate on the phone (from speed, acceleration and slope), for reference rides without a power meter, and for the speed on the trainer.

CdA 0.32 is riding on the hoods, about 0.28 in the drops. Crr 0.0055 is good road tyres; 0.008 rough roads.

Button test: press a button on a Bluetooth remote (or the phone's volume button). Page Down / → / Enter = Reset gap; Page Up / ← = Brake (trainer and video screens).

My rides

Every ride is kept on this device while you ride (the last 30). Save FIT puts it in Downloads, for Garmin Connect (website → Import Data) or Strava. Use as reference lets you race it next time.

Offline map

Stores the streets around where you are now on this phone, so the map works on rides without internet. Do it at home on Wi-Fi. On rides with internet, missing parts load by themselves.

Panels

Drag a panel by its title, resize it at the bottom-right corner. Positions are kept on this computer.

Reference ride

Pick the original ride's .fit file, or the .zip straight from Garmin Connect (the activity → ⚙ → Export Original). Rides without a power meter get power estimated from speed, grade and weight, as in tools/estimate_power.py.

Ride Display

Tablet: connect the HR strap and the trainer (both over Bluetooth; the watch keeps the trainer on ANT+), press Start as you start the watch.
Phone: connect the HR strap and GPS, press Start at the spot where the original ride started.
Reset gap lines the ghost up with you again, like the Lap button on the watch; with gloves, use a button (see Settings → Button test).
